## About me

I left my family and friends at 16 to go to boarding school at The Velammal International School. The days there were built around rigorous, structured study, and it was a lot. It was also where computer science stopped being just another subject and became the thing I chose to spend my free time on.

That carried me to the University of Illinois Chicago, where I study Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics, and into two summers of internships. In Chennai, I worked full-stack on a shipping and logistics product with Java, Angular, and PostgreSQL. In Abu Dhabi, at AD Ports Group, I worked on ATLP, the emirate's digital trade and logistics platform: Python and SQL pipelines for operational event data, analytics layers behind service-health dashboards, and API telemetry. Most of the projects below come from seeing how much real operational work still runs on spreadsheets and manual reports.

I also build things just because I want them to exist, like a real-time music visualizer in Rust. I'm open to software engineering internships and full-time roles, especially in backend, systems, and data-heavy tools.
